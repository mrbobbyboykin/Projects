About

Your vision, elevated.

Events by ElainaB specializes in concept-to-completion planning for clients who want more than decor — they want an experience. Every celebration is tailored to reflect your personality, your story, and the feeling you want guests to carry home.

Whether you need full event coordination or help with a single focal point, we bring creativity, organization, and a meticulous eye for detail to every project across the DMV.
